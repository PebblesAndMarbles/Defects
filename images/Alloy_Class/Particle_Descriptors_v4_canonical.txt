CANONICAL PARTICLE DESCRIPTOR SPEC (v4) -- ready for prompt construction.
Supersedes Particle_Descriptors_v3_canonical.txt and the v4 draft.

1a. Coarse Shape -- SINGLE-select
Circle
Round
Angular
Clumped

Calibration guidance:
  - Circle: reserve for a close-to-true circular/disc outline only -- smooth and
    symmetric all the way around, with no flat/faceted segment or notch anywhere on
    the boundary. Max acceptable deviation: one subtle, flatter facet on one
    portion of an otherwise round outline.
    Prefer Round or Angular when not clearly circular rather than defaulting to
    Circle under uncertainty. 
  - Round: soft/blobby outline, no circle-level regularity and no straight/angular
    edges.
  - Angular: at least one relatively straight edge or corner-like feature is visible.
  - Clumped: reserve for ONE outline that itself looks fused/multi-lobed (multiple
    bumps/lobes on a single connected silhouette, ambiguous whether it's one particle
    or several merged). Do not use Clumped just because defect_count > 1 with clearly
    separate, individually-outlined particles -- describe each particle's own shape
    instead.

2b. Fine Texture -- independent True/False
Layered
Interior_Striation
Fractured
Scraggly

Fractured trigger definition: a visible, irregular gap/break separating or nearly
separating two to four parts of the particle body -- distinct from Layered (a line
that follows a fold or a comparator edge) and Interior_Striation (straight internal
lines), and explicitly not just general rough/craggy surface appearance (that's
Scraggly, or coarse_texture=Rough).

Scraggly trigger definition: overall craggy, nodular, multi-bump surface across the
particle body -- many small knobs/lobes giving a cauliflower-like impression,
without one single identifiable break (Fractured) or a fold/boundary-following line
(Layered).

3. Aggregation/Count
defect_count -- quantitative int. Known to sometimes undercount fused/clumped
particles as a single defect; coarse_shape=Clumped is the intended hedge for that
case (see 1a).

4. Size
size_percent_of_image -- quantitative float, model's visual estimate of defect area
as a percent of total image area. Binning into categorical size labels is deferred
until the distribution across a larger sample is known.

5. Position
location_relative -- single-select: on_sio / in_comparator / spanning_both / unclear.
  - on_sio: defect either (1) does not touch a comparator's real or inferred boundary
    at all, or (2) touches that boundary but does not cross over/into it (stays fully
    on the SiO side).
  - in_comparator: defect is fully within a comparator's real or inferred boundary.
  - spanning_both: defect's own edge crosses at least one comparator's real or
    inferred boundary line (part of the defect on the SiO side, part on the
    comparator side).

6. Image Quality
focus_quality -- single-select: in_focus / out_of_focus / mixed.
