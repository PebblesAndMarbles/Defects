1a. Coarse Shape -- SINGLE-select (unchanged from v3)
Circle
Round
Angular
Clumped

Calibration guidance (unchanged from v3):
  - Circle: reserve for a close-to-true circular/disc outline only -- smooth and
    symmetric all the way around, with no flat/faceted segment or notch anywhere on
    the boundary. Max acceptable deviation: one distinctly flatter facet on one
    portion of an otherwise round outline (reference: AME425_PM5 D6231600 W023 ID1).
    Model confidence on "circle" calls in v2 was measurably lower than other
    coarse_shape calls (e.g. 0.78 vs. a typical 0.86-0.96) -- prefer Round or Angular
    when not clearly circular rather than defaulting to Circle under uncertainty.
  - Round: soft/blobby outline, no circle-level regularity and no straight/angular
    edges.
  - Angular: at least one relatively straight edge or corner-like feature is visible.
  - Clumped: reserve for ONE outline that itself looks fused/multi-lobed (multiple
    bumps/lobes on a single connected silhouette, ambiguous whether it's one particle
    or several merged). Do not use Clumped just because defect_count > 1 with clearly
    separate, individually-outlined particles -- describe each particle's own shape
    in that case (defect_count already captures the multiplicity).

[NOTE, not a spec change] v3 called "circle" on only 1 of 40 cases, and that one case
was still judged a miss on review -- this pilot set genuinely contains zero real
circles. We cannot yet confirm the tightened calibration is working correctly (only
that it suppressed a shape that may never have belonged here); revisit once a larger
sample includes actual circular particles.

2b. Fine Texture -- independent True/False

[CHANGE] Porous removed -- never fired in v2 or v3 (0/40 both runs); coarse_texture
=Rough already covers what it was meant to capture, so it added a field with no
observed discriminating value.

Fractured trigger definition [TIGHTENED from v3]: a visible, irregular gap/break
separating or nearly separating two to four parts of the particle body -- distinct from
Layered (a line that follows a fold or a comparator edge) and Interior_Striation
(straight internal lines), and explicitly NOT just "the surface looks rough/craggy
overall" (that's Scraggly, see below, or coarse_texture=Rough). Reference examples:
SMP_PILOT_036 and SMP_PILOT_011 both show a real, visible discontinuity and should
trigger this; SMP_PILOT_020 was a weak/incorrect call under the old undefined
version -- its craggy-all-over surface has no single identifiable break and should
NOT trigger Fractured under this tightened definition.

Scraggly trigger definition [NEW]: overall craggy, nodular, multi-bump surface
across the particle body -- many small knobs/lobes giving a cauliflower-like
impression, without one single identifiable break (that's Fractured) or a fold/
boundary-following line (that's Layered). Reference examples, all judged the same
morphology: SMP_PILOT_020, SMP_PILOT_040, SMP_PILOT_005, plus two references outside
the test set (AME425_PM1 D618204 W128 ID16 -- same image as SMP_PILOT_040; AME411_PM5
D619313 W481 ID155).

3. Aggregation/Count (unchanged from v3)
defect_count -- quantitative int. Known to sometimes undercount fused/clumped
particles as a single defect; coarse_shape=Clumped is the intended hedge for that
case (see 1a).

4. Size
size_percent_of_image (unchanged from v3) -- quantitative float, model's visual
estimate of defect area as a percent of total image area. Binning into categorical
size labels is deferred until the distribution across a larger sample is known.

[CHANGE] Comparator_Fit REMOVED -- returned "yes" on 40/40 cases in both v2 and v3,
zero discriminating value observed across both runs.

5. Position (unchanged from v3)
location_relative -- single-select: on_sio / in_comparator / spanning_both / unclear.
  - on_sio: defect either (1) does not touch a comparator's real or inferred boundary
    at all, or (2) touches that boundary but does not cross over/into it (stays fully
    on the SiO side).
  - in_comparator: defect is fully within a comparator's real or inferred boundary.
  - spanning_both: defect's own edge crosses at least one comparator's real or
    inferred boundary line (part of the defect on the SiO side, part on the
    comparator side).

6. Image Quality (unchanged from v3)
focus_quality -- single-select: in_focus / out_of_focus / mixed.
